Our story

Slow food, made by people who care about the details

Pistachio began in 2019 as a three-table espresso bar. Today we're a full kitchen, but we've kept the same rule: everything is made like it's for someone we know.

What we're working toward

We're not chasing being the biggest café in Accra — we're chasing being the one people trust with their mornings.

  • Source honestly

    Work directly with local farms and roasters, and always know exactly where our ingredients come from.

  • Cut food waste in half

    Plan our daily bakes around real demand, and turn surplus into staff meals and community donations.

  • Grow our own team

    Train from within — most of our senior kitchen staff started as apprentices in this very building.

Where we've gotten to so far

What we hold onto

The things that don't change as we grow.

Hospitality first

Every plate leaves the kitchen the way we'd want to receive it ourselves — warm, unhurried, and generous.

Made fresh, not fast

We'd rather you wait a few extra minutes than eat something that was made in advance and reheated.

Honest sourcing

We name the farms our produce comes from, because you deserve to know what you're eating and who made it.

A place for everyone

Solo laptop mornings, big family brunches, first dates — every table gets the same care.

A few common questions

Do you take reservations?

We hold a handful of tables for walk-ins every day, but for groups of 5 or more we recommend calling ahead so we can seat you comfortably.

Can you accommodate allergies or dietary needs?

Yes — most of our menu can be adapted. Add a note at checkout or let your server know, and our kitchen will take it from there.

Do you deliver outside East Legon?

Right now we deliver within a 6km radius of the café. Enter your address at checkout and we'll let you know if you're in range.

Is parking available?

There's free parking directly behind the building, with space for about 15 cars.

Come see us, or order in

Whichever way you'd rather have your morning, we've got you.
